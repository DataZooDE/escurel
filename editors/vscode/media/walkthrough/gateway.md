## Point Escurel at your gateway

Escurel reads and writes your knowledge through a gateway (an `escurel-server`).
Set its address once, in your **user** settings (a project's own settings cannot change it, so a
repository can never redirect where your token goes).

Open the setting with the button, enter e.g. `http://127.0.0.1:8080` or your team's URL.
